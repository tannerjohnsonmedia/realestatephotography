05 How it goes

From first spark to final cut.

  1. 01

    Strike

    A call and a real conversation. We dig until we find the one idea your film is actually about.

  2. 02

    Kindle

    Pre-production. Treatment, script, shot list, locations, crew and a schedule you can hold us to.

  3. 03

    Burn

    Shoot day. Cinema cameras, real lighting, clean sound, and a crew that moves fast without rushing you.

  4. 04

    Glow

    Edit, color grade, sound design and delivery in every size you need — from the big screen to the story.
