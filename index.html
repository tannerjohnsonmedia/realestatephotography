    Questions

    Everything else.

    How fast do I get my photos?

    Next business day for standard bookings. If you're up against a listing deadline, say so when you book — same-day delivery is often possible.

    Do I need to be at the shoot?

    No. We can coordinate access with the seller or the lockbox directly. Plenty of agents never meet us on site.

    What should the seller do to prepare?

    Lights on, blinds open, counters clear, cars out of the driveway, and pet items put away. We'll send a short prep checklist when you book.

    Is drone footage included?

    Aerial coverage is included in the Premium and Ultimate video packages, and can be added to any photo package. All flights are FAA-compliant.

    Can I use the photos after the listing sells?

    Yes — you get full usage rights for marketing the listing and your own brand, including social, print, and your website.

    What if the weather is bad?

    We'll reschedule at no charge, or shoot interiors and return for exteriors on the next clear day. No listing goes live with a gray-sky front elevation.

    Do you offer discounts for volume?

    Yes. If you're listing consistently, call and we'll set up repeat-agent pricing.
