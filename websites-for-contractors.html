The problem

Most contractor websites lose jobs quietly.

You get the work through referrals and reviews. Then people look you up, and the website decides whether they call you or the next company.

  • It's slow or broken on a phone.Most people searching for a contractor are on their phone. If the page is slow or the text is tiny, they leave.
  • There's no obvious next step.If a visitor has to hunt for your number or a quote form, many won't bother.
  • It's all stock photos.Generic images say nothing about the quality of your work. Real projects build trust faster than any slogan.
  • It doesn't show up in local search.No service pages, no neighborhoods, and a Google listing that doesn't match the site makes you harder to find.
